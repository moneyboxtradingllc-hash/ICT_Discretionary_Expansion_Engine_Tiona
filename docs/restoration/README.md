## Safety changes

Affirmative entry intent must be explicit (propose_entry, historical enter alias);
watching remains a conditional plan and cannot become an immediate order. This
replaces the historical accidental permission for any unrecognized prose. Only
the prompt's action representation changes; original analysis, tools, opportunity
law and risk/management policy remain historical.

The original watching-plan zone, aware expiry and trade identity are copied to a
final-quote validator through ProductionLoop -> ProductionSession.build_runner ->
ExecutionRunner.gated_submit. It rechecks after economics before minting too.
No new market authority or recognition requirement is created. Original contract,
stop, objective, exact tool occurrence, playbook and family cannot be replaced.

Journal fixes use the actual loop-owned CandidateProducer and only this execution's
reported quote/economics/submission evidence. They cannot create a candidate or
change an order. A missing submission timestamp is never logged as submission.

exercise candidate authorization. Original tests remain immutable at cbfaddb and
initial frozen run failures are retained in the owner evidence package.

Fingerprint pins deliberately move from brain:0ec4ef07c75dbcef to
brain:2144f0a579721394 for the affirmative-action safety repair; old authorizations

The replay walker environment-restoration assertion now compares the exact prior

were made for that test portability issue.

same records and assertions pass at the fixture date; explicit expired-record

are test-clock corrections, not a policy expansion.

## Evidence limits

Controlled Brain responses prove software pathways, not natural discretionary
recognition or profitability. Historical reports of Oct-1 11:46 long and Oct-2
10:43:46 watching plan are corroborating correspondence, not full original
session reconstructions. The unsafe Oct-1 verbose-watching short is expressly
excluded as a recovered capability. Missing operator-only archives or a live
memory corpus must remain visible as missing evidence, not be fabricated or
replaced by synthetic production records.

Do not arm while the consolidated owner audit reports unresolved safety or
acceptance blockers. Operational cutover needs fresh source/model/retrieval,
account/contract, consumed-allowance, order/position and session checks with a
separate explicit owner authorization. An isolated source clone is not an
operator-state migration.
