# Frozen baseline and controlled evolution

Owner policy effective October 29, 2026: one strategic change at a time, followed
by five full eligible trading sessions, ordinarily one market week, at the same
frozen code/configuration. This supersedes earlier proposed observation intervals.
Five sessions is a minimum review interval, not statistical proof of profitability.
Inconclusive samples remain inconclusive; continue unchanged observation where
needed. A quiet session or losing trade is not grounds for architecture surgery.

Begin restored observation with non-ordering/simulated operation after offline
acceptance. Any Combine ordering needs separate session authorization and normal
preflight. No observation days or trading permissions are claimed by this package.

Record a source/config/fingerprint identity and complete session coverage, scans,
Brain attempts/successes/failures/timeouts, candidates, qualification outcomes and
exact refusal owner/reason, plans, entry attempts, fills and management events.
For each trade record contemporaneous type/timeframe/direction, structural stop,
target, quantity, realized gross/net P&L, commissions and measured slippage.
Calculate average gain/loss, win rate, payoff, net expectancy and drawdown only
from complete reconciled fills and costs; zero denominator is UNDEFINED, not 0.
Report no opportunity, voluntary stand-down, legitimate qualification refusal,
unintended restriction, and cognition/provider failure separately. Repeated scans
of one episode are not independent opportunities. Do not infer setups from later
profits. Preserve evidence gaps as UNKNOWN.

Every proposed strategy change gets its own specification, commit, focused
regression evidence, forward interval and documented disposition before another
begins. Safety interventions are identified separately. If an intervention changes
trading behavior materially, start a new clean observation baseline. Do not erase
the earlier period or its consumed allowances.

experiment; contemporaneous authoritative countertrend-retracement-scalp
classification demonstrated at entry and propagated to the management consumer.

No such reliable dedicated classification has yet been established in the restored
source. Candidate `extras.playbook`, Brain `narrative_phase`, and tool families
are present; none alone proves a bounded countertrend scalp versus a reversal.
Do not infer the classification from P&L, price moving against an earlier bias,
or a tool name. This is an explicit prerequisite gap, not permission to add a
classification engine in this mission.

If that prerequisite is subsequently satisfied, one isolated commit sets only
eligible scalp profit objective to entry +/-30.0 MNQ index points, respecting
existing absolute-price/tick alignment and existing execution conventions.
This is 120 ticks at 0.25, not 30 ticks. No 25-point alternative in the same
experiment. Keep entry signals, qualification, structural stop, sizing/caps,
fees and risk budgets unchanged. Re-evaluate original reward/cost eligibility;
a smaller objective cannot promote an unqualified opportunity. If the 30-point
objective conflicts with existing authoritative management/geometry, reject
that experiment candidate rather than silently changing doctrine.

classification must predate outcome. No stop widening, higher frequency, fixed

unchanged entries/stops/size, non-scalp invariance, refusal controls, restart and

sessions and compare the frozen versions using forward evidence with market
conditions reported. No profitability claim from either small interval.
